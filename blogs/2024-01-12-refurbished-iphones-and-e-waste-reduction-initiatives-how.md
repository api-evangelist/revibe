Introduction Electronic waste, or "e-waste," has grown to be a serious environmental issue in our rapidly changing digital age. The issue is getting worse as smartphones, like the well-known iPhone, become more and more common. Thankfully, Apple, a major worldwide tech company, is not only changing how we use our gadgets but also taking the lead in efforts to reduce e-waste.
